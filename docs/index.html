Fair Plus-Minus

Fair Plus-Minus is a player impact metric built for the EuroLeague. It estimates how much a player helps his team outscore the opponent per 100 possessions, once you account for the teammates next to him and the players he is up against.

It starts from the box score, checks it against what the scoreboard actually did with him on the floor, and grades every shot by how good it was rather than whether it went in. Each season is judged against its own league, all the way back to 2007‑08, and the number is split into an offensive and a defensive half.
