Fair Plus-Minus, or FPM, is a player impact metric for the EuroLeague. It's built around a basic question: when a player is on the floor, does his team outscore the other one, and how much of that is actually down to him?

PIR, the league's official number, doesn't really answer that. It adds up the good things, takes away the bad ones, and never asks who else was on the court or whether the team won those minutes. FPM starts from the box score as well, but it checks it against the scoreboard, pulls a player apart from his teammates and opponents, and judges shots by how good they were rather than by whether they went in.

The number is points per 100 possessions compared to an average player. Zero is average, +3 or so gets you into a season's top 20, and the very best end up around +6 or +7. Every season since 2007‑08 is on the leaderboard, and the current one keeps updating as the games are played.

If you want to know how it works, or where it gets things wrong, it's all on the about page. The data and the code that collects it are in the public repository, free for anyone to use.
